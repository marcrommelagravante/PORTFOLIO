# Mandatory Rules & Guidelines

## Skill Utilization Priority (Mandatory)

Always check and activate relevant workspace skills before designing, writing, refactoring, or reviewing code:

1. **Frontend Design, Polish & UX**:
   - `ui-ux-pro-max` ([`.agents/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/SKILL.md`](.agents/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/SKILL.md)): Color palettes, typography pairings, layout hierarchies, component architecture, and interaction guidelines.
   - `ui-taste` ([`.agents/skills/ui-taste/SKILL.md`](.agents/skills/ui-taste/SKILL.md)) & `design-taste-frontend` ([`.agents/skills/design-taste-frontend/SKILL.md`](.agents/skills/design-taste-frontend/SKILL.md)): Anti-UI-slop playbooks, visual refinement, spacing, rhythm, micro-interactions, and high-fidelity polish.

2. **Clean Engineering & Simplicity**:
   - `ponytail` ([`.agents/skills/ponytail/SKILL.md`](.agents/skills/ponytail/SKILL.md)): The smallest, cleanest, high-impact change that fully solves the problem. Avoid over-engineering, unneeded abstractions, and bloat (YAGNI).

3. **Mobile & iOS UI/UX**:
   - `ios-design` ([`.agents/skills/ios-design/SKILL.md`](.agents/skills/ios-design/SKILL.md)): Apple HIG standards, safe area layout, typography, navigation flows, and gestures.

4. **Chinese Copy & Localization**:
   - `humanizer-zh` ([`.agents/skills/humanizer-zh/SKILL.md`](.agents/skills/humanizer-zh/SKILL.md)): Eliminate repetitive, robotic, or template-like phrasing, maintaining a natural, clear voice.

5. **Tool & Platform Skills**:
   - Use applicable skills (e.g., `chrome-devtools` for web debugging, performance audits, and accessibility) whenever matching tasks arise.
